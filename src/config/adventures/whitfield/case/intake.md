# Catherine Whitfield — Intake

**Presenting:** Concert pianist, mid-40s. Severely impacted by stage fright that has only been presenting for the last eighteen months. No evidence of a physical condition contributing to the her problem.  Her performance ability and technique are fully intact as evidenced during practice.  The issue appears to be purely psychological.

## Background

Catherine has played in front of audiences around the world for thirty years without any issues. She now feels such significant anxiety prior to a performance in front of an audience that she invariably experience an anxiety attack. Her pulse races, she feels nauseous and becomes faint.

During her preparatory psychotherapy sessions it became clear that the onset of symptoms coincided with after a recital at the Conservatory where she was originally a student. This recital was intended to be a celebration of an illustrious and successful alumni.  Unfortunately it has inadvertently left her in serious distress that is impacting her career.

During therapy she has also described a recurrent dream that is likely to play a role in any dream walk that is undertaken.  She describes the dream as taking place in *a cavernous concert hall*. There is an audience that is *stern and unmoving* waiting expectantly for her to perform.  The wings of the stage are dark and foreboding.  In her dream she has been unabel to venture into the wings due to an unnameed fear. When she attempts to perform the piano keys resist her and no sound is produced.  This leads her to wake in terror.

## Notes

- No physical infirmity. Her hands are fine. She experiences no tremors or pain.
- Her ability and technique is intact. She practises daily and the practice is unimpaired.
- She has a recurring dream that seems to be a symbolic representation of the underlying cause of her anxiety.
