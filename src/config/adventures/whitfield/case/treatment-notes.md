# Treatment notes

** Dr Elias Wren**

Mrs Whitfield has undertaken extensive therapy with a well respected Psychiatrist but has not experienced significant improvement. She was referred to the Wren Institute by a relative who was successfully treated three years ago.

Since arriving, I have conducted a number of traditional therapy sessions with her to gain an understanding of her condition and to ascertain the possible causes.

## Hypothesis of underlying cause

My hypothesis is that Mrs Whitfield experienced some an unresolved trauma during her time studying at the Conservatory that she is no longer consciously aware of, or willing to raise.

This hypothesis is supported by the her issues being triggered by a return to the Conservatory and by the general feelings of fear and foreboding she describes when discussing her recurring dream.

If this is the case then I suspect the trauma is related to the training she underwent while at the Conservatroy as her anxiety is clearly focussed on her piano playing.  The fact that her issues are tied to public performance, rather than piano playing in general, points to the trauma being related her performance experiences at the Conservatory.

## Considerations for dream therapy

When conducting a dream walk with Catherine the following should be considered:
* Uncovering the root of her anxiety could be quite distressing.  There may be a need to be gentle
* If she is repressing the root of the problem then it will likely be repressed in her dream too
* The fact that she can still play without issue indicates the piano will feature in the dream but may not be significant
